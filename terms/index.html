Passports, visas & travel documents

You are responsible for making sure every traveller has the right documents for the whole journey, including transit points. This includes:

  • A valid passport. Many countries require at least six months' validity beyond your return date, and blank pages.
  • The visas, transit visas and entry permits you need.
  • Any health, vaccination or other entry requirements of the countries you are visiting.
  • Names on bookings that match your passport exactly.

We can help you with visa applications and give general guidance. However, embassies and authorities make all visa decisions, and we cannot guarantee that a visa will be granted or issued on time. Visa fees and our visa assistance fee are non-refundable if a visa is refused.

We are not responsible for costs or losses if you are refused boarding or entry because of missing, invalid or incorrect documents.
